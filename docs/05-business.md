# 05. Business

## The problem

Universities, innovation hubs, corporates and development partners run innovation challenges every year. Most run them on Google Forms, spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and Canva certificates. That means:

- applications scattered across forms and email
- judges scoring in separate spreadsheets that someone has to merge by hand
- no record of who participated across events
- certificates that cannot be verified
- weak reporting for funders who want numbers on reach and impact

## Customers

| Segment | Examples | What they care about | Who pays |
|---|---|---|---|
| Universities | Innovation offices, CS and business faculties, student guilds | Running many events a year, student records, reputation | Innovation office budget |
| Innovation hubs | University hubs like MUTIC, independent tech hubs | Pipeline of startups, sponsor reporting, looking professional | Hub ops budget, often grant-funded |
| Corporates | Banks, telecoms, insurers running open innovation or internal ideation | Branding, talent scouting, confidentiality | Marketing / innovation / HR |
| Development partners | NGOs, UN agencies, donor-funded programmes | Impact reporting (gender, region, institution), transparent judging, audit trail | Programme budgets, often per-event |

Development partners are the most valuable early segment: they run frequent challenges, have budgets attached to each one, and must report numbers to donors. Transparent judging and impact reports are a selling point for them.

## Pricing (draft, to validate with real customers)

| Plan | Price | For | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Student clubs, trying it out | 1 active challenge, 50 participants, Kakushin branding on certificates |
| Hub | $29/month (~UGX 105k) | Hubs and university departments | 3 active challenges, 500 participants, own logo on certificates |
| Institution | $99/month (~UGX 360k) | Universities and large hubs | Unlimited challenges, 5,000 participants, multiple admins, exports |
| Partner / Enterprise | Custom, or $300 to $1,500 per event | Corporates and development partners | Impact reports, custom domain, priority support, data export, invoicing |

UGX figures are approximate and will be set in local currency on launch.

Also offer a **per-event price** because many development partners buy per programme, not per month.

Payments (later phase): Flutterwave or Pesapal for mobile money (MTN, Airtel) and cards, plus manual invoicing for institutions that pay by bank transfer.

## Running cost

| Stage | Monthly cost |
|---|---|
| Pilot | ~$0 (Cloudflare free + Resend free), plus a domain (~$10 to $15/year) |
| First paying customers | ~$5 Workers Paid + $20 Resend Pro |

Margins are very high because the infrastructure is almost free at this scale.

## Go-to-market

1. Run one real challenge for free with a friendly hub or university club, and collect a testimonial.
2. Use that as a case study to pitch other hubs and innovation offices in Kampala.
3. Approach development partners who already fund challenges, leading with impact reports and transparent judging.
4. Each event exposes the platform to hundreds of participants, who become future organisers, judges and referrals.

## What makes it stick

- Participant profiles that build up across events (a portable record of hackathons and certificates)
- Verifiable certificates that participants share on LinkedIn, which markets the platform for free
- Historical data that organisers do not want to lose
